## Who We Are
Cami Technologies Limited is a SaaS / business operations software company for service businesses. Cami streamlines appointment-based business operations in the UAE and GCC regions through WhatsApp-native technology.

## What We Do
Cami provides an integrated platform that consolidates customer communications, booking management, automated reminders, and payment processing for appointment-based service businesses, all inside WhatsApp, so operators never switch between apps.

## Positioning
In market, Cami leads with **Cami Pay** and **Cami CRM**, not "booking software." Booking alone does not differentiate and does not sell; the payments and CRM framing is the wedge. WhatsApp on its own is not rich enough, Cami fixes a problem WhatsApp cannot fix itself and turns the resulting data into value for the business.

## Mission
Streamline appointment-based business operations in the UAE and GCC regions through WhatsApp-native technology, giving service businesses one place to communicate, book, remind, and get paid.

## What We Believe
- **Solve a day-0 pain, not sell an OS.** Customers want WhatsApp under control, not an operating system. WhatsApp-native AI scheduling leads acquisition; the OS prevents churn. Once scheduling, payments, reminders, and records all live in Cami, there's nothing to switch back to.
- **The checkout is the business.** The OS is free; revenue is margin on payments captured through bookings. One funnel, no second onboarding to turn on payments.
- **Meet the customer where they already are.** Booking happens on WhatsApp, day and night. That conversational channel is the front door, not a bolt-on integration.

## Values
- **The customer never fights our tools.** Booking, paying, and rebooking all happen in the thread the customer is already in. No app to download, no link to chase.
- **Instant, always-on service.** The AI Receptionist answers in seconds, day or night, so no inquiry goes cold and no booking is lost to a missed message.
- **One product, not a toolbox.** Scheduling, payments, reminders, and records are one experience. The business runs on Cami, not a stack of stitched-together apps.
- **Built for how the region actually works.** Multi-branch by default, WhatsApp-first, and ready for the languages and rails GCC customers use.

## Market Context
**Industry:** SaaS / Business operations software for service businesses

**Target Market:** Cami is for general, appointment-heavy service businesses in the UAE and KSA (and the wider GCC): beauty salons, spas, wellness centers, clinics, barbershops, fitness studios, pet businesses, and other schedule-dense verticals. The goal is one platform that any appointment-based service can run on.

**Historical note:** In v0, Cami started with pet services for its earliest beachhead customers. Through v0.1, a natural pull toward other appointment services emerged, which is why the current framing is general appointment-heavy services rather than pet-only.

## Business Model
Payments-led, not subscription-led. The OS is free to businesses; Cami earns a **percentage of payments processed** through Cami Pay (deposits, balances, rebooks). The live payment partner is **NeoPay**; CamiPay routes through a provider abstraction (NI / NeoPay / Stripe), so Cami can onboard more providers over time as preferential rates and customer ease-of-use warrant. **Add-on pricing** (WhatsApp and reminder monetization) layers on top of the processing margin. No SaaS subscription floor at v1, the checkout is the business.

## Company Stage

Live since **July 1, 2026**, monetizing from month 1. Four service operators (roughly $250K total monthly transaction value) onboarding over the first 5 to 6 weeks. Business is in early go-to-market: MVP live at Tier 3, Tier 2 (SOTA) go-live 10 August, Tier 1 (Chaps & Co) for Q4 2026. **UAE is the live market; KSA is a target but gated on a Saudi-resident data stack** (data-sovereignty work) before launch.

## Team & Org

- **Structure:** CEO over three functions, Technology, Product, and Commercial. Engineering splits into an **OS Team** (Core OS) and a **MOAT Team** (CRM and AI features, Cami's moat), plus Mobile App, Sales, and Customer Success.
- **Hiring:** 2x Senior Full-Stack Engineer (Tech), 1x Sales Development, 1x Customer Success (Commercial).
- **Direction:** Moving from an outsourced engineering model to an in-house product and engineering function for faster, more agile delivery. Commercial capacity (BD and account management) is being brought in earlier to hit the end-of-year target.
