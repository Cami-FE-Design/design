# Cami Q3 2026 Product Roadmap (August to September)
## Context
*Pulled from your files, the Linear sync, and the Jul 31 meetings:*

- **Strategic priorities (goals.md):** Land Tier 2 (SOTA first), scale the active base, hit reforecast economics.
- **Current roadmap (product.md):** Version track v0.1 (land SOTA via CamiPay + reminders) into v0.2 (conversational WhatsApp + AI Receptionist, Aug to Sep). Post-SOTA priority is multi-location.
- **User pain (personas.md):** Receptionist drowning in WhatsApp, forgets deposit links, no-shows leak revenue. SOTA is churning off Fresha.
- **Competitive (competitors.md):** Fresha and ezyVet named as the two real competitors; the wedge is WhatsApp CRM + integrated payments, which neither has. Clinical vets deliberately not chased now.

## Planning Context
**Planning Period:** Q3 2026 (August to September). Q4 items (Tier 1, v1 GA) are under Later.

**Team Capacity:** In transition. Moving from outsourced engineering to in-house; hiring 2x Senior Full-Stack Engineer, 1x SDR, 1x Customer Success, plus a local commercial AE. ⚠️ Treat mid-quarter in-house transition as a delivery risk, not added throughput.

> **Note on the no-show outcome:** the current NeoPay setup does not store card, so there is no card-on-file and therefore no automatic no-show fee. The ~30% reduction is achieved behaviorally (timely confirmations and reminders drive attendance), not through a financial penalty. We can still evidence the metric through attendance data; the no-show-fee capability would require card-on-file, which is not in scope with the current payment setup.

**Why Now:** Everything here is a switch-blocker for SOTA's 10 August go-live, the first Tier 2 win. Payments is the critical path but splits: **online links (built, testing) go first**, the **card terminal is still in architecture and gated on NeoPay** (settlement + terminal-app decisions + approval), so online-first is the interim path. WhatsApp reminders are the goal but blocked on META, so SMS + email carry reminders at go-live.

**Why Next:** **Multi-location is the named adoption driver** once SOTA is stable, the system is single-location today and Chaps & Co has 9. Multi-location plus group bookings and boarding unlocks new categories (pilates, group-booking and boarding businesses) and the path to enterprise, and stress-tests the scheduling engine so it is proven generic across pet and non-pet. Conversational WhatsApp is the Fresha-switch differentiator but depends on META. Tier 2 pet targets boarding businesses, not clinical vets.
