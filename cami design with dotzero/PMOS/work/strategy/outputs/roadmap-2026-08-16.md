### Settlement: the deck and the PRD are describing different things

The 31 Jul meeting says a settlement process **already exists**:

> NeoPay same-day settles online money to **Crescent Enterprise**. Crescent then pays out to each merchant, **manually**. Merchants receive money ~5 days later or weekly. This is an **interim solution to not lose launch time**, and "Crescent's process adds slowness and needs to change."

So "Cami Pay Settlements, August" most likely means **keep the manual Crescent path running and build the payment log**, not the automated lifecycle in my PRD. Those are very different sizes of work.

**This correction goes back into the PRD:** I wrote payout-run as "🔴 nothing exists". Wrong. A manual process exists, GNK is building the log, and Veal owns the reconciliation loop. The PRD should describe **automating an existing manual process**, which is a much better-defined problem.

**Decide before sprint planning:** does August mean (a) the log plus manual payouts, or (b) the automated payout run? If (b), the two blockers from the PRD stand: how Cami collects its fee on terminal, and whether holding float needs a licence.

## Recommended changes to the plan

1. **Cut August from six to three.** Keep CamiPay Online, POS Terminal, and Settlements. Hold Agentic AI Platform and Dynamic Pricing. Reporting stays only if the ADR-024 conditions close this week.
2. **Decide the settlement scope this week.** Log-plus-manual is achievable in August. The automated run is not, and pretending otherwise costs the September plan too.
3. **Promote Boarding Calendar or kill the pipeline.** Six operators waiting on an undated item is the clearest revenue-per-effort case on this page.
4. **Move Migration out of Later.** It is the mechanism that makes Fresha churn repeatable, which is the Tier 2 engine.
5. **Close EC-18 at ~3x the Tier 2 GMV floor** and correct the derived figures in the JTBD and settlement PRD.
6. **Get a date or a decision on SOTA.** Everything downstream assumes it.
