### Rewrites for the three weak ones

**OBJ-P1 → controllable form.** Do not make an external approval a key result.
> Original: "Unibox live once META unblocks."
> Rewrite: "Business Verification submitted and escalated through both META contacts by 31 Aug; Coexistence decision (yes/no, owner, staffing hours, whether pilot operators are told) recorded by 31 Aug; when the chain clears, Unibox live within 14 days."
> **Rationale:** the submission, the escalation, and the bridge decision are Cami's. The approval is not. Score the parts you own.

**OBJ-P2 → outcome form.**
> Original: "Make the Core OS feature-rich for a 30-staff Tier 2 operator."
> Rewrite: "One 30-staff operator runs a full week on Cami with no parallel system: ≥95% of their appointments booked in Cami, ≥35% of their GMV captured on Cami rails, zero days on a spreadsheet."
> **Rationale:** "feature-rich" is a judgment. "Ran a week without falling back to Fresha" is a fact, and it is the actual Tier 2 proof point.

**OBJ-P3 → decide it, then measure it.**
> Rewrite: "Confirm or retire self-serve onboarding by 31 Aug. If confirmed: N self-serve signups reach first captured payment without a human, by 30 Nov."
> **Rationale:** OBJ-B2's 36 businesses has no acquisition motion behind it in the deck. If self-serve is dead, 36 needs a different mechanism named.
