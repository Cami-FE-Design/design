## The five flows walked

1. **Link unknown inbound → existing profile.** Unmatched WhatsApp number can be linked to a Cami client profile in one click, right from the inbox. Once linked, surfaces history: last 3 appointments, notes, "usual staff." Saves ~1.5 to 2.5 min of copy-paste per booking (Cami's own time-cost estimate).
2. **Create profile from inbox.** New/random contact → create profile in-thread (quick-create or full intake), no leaving the inbox.
3. **AI booking flow / close-the-gap.** AI suggests slots that fill calendar gaps, surfaces preferred staff + alternatives, then fires the deposit link on booking as the confirmation step. Removes the manual calendar-gap hunt.
4. **Message triage / ranking.** AI ranks conversations by urgency (unanswered, deposit due, cancellation window closing, complaints/escalations) into buckets Queenie can clear fast in the morning.
5. **Deposits & reminders triage card.** Auto-surfaced template card tags Queenie on conversations needing action (chase confirmations/deposit links), triaged in minutes vs hours.

**North-star metric proposed:** book-to-confirm conversion. Sample: roughly half of inbound became bookings, and the large majority of those confirmed = **82% conversion**. "If this number is up, we're successful."

## Key requirements & insights from Queenie (raw signal)

**"Usual staff" logic**
- Don't over-engineer. Rule = **count of appointments with the same staff**; most recent wins. Client transfers only when regular is on vacation, then returns.
- Placeholder "usual staff" needs config help from operator (logic differs: hair → stylist, nails → nail master). Keep the card **short** so it's fast to read.
- Goal: maximize one client doing everything in the salon (cross-service), so simple beats a rigid "preferred stylist" field like old software.

**Multi-service / group bookings (the hard part)**
- Clients often book hair + nails + facial together. Salon actively upsells same-day.
- **Service dictates the schedule, not the stylist.** Service value ranks: hair takes primary (e.g. 15 min color/blow-dry before nails). Sequence vs parallel matters.
- Current screen only shows one start time; unclear how it handles parallel/sequential multi-service. Flagged as a **UI problem**, engine stays same, more cards to pick from.
- Ask: surface previously-bundled services as a rebooking suggestion ("reorder the same order you love"). Regulars repeat similar appointments.

**Rescheduling is the real workload (bigger than new bookings)**
- Most conversation volume = modifying existing appointments, not creating new ones.
- To fit a client, Queenie reshuffles: moves appointments, asks stylist to extend hours, squeezes walk-ins. Judgment call driven by **who's a big spender / VIP**.
- Reschedule triggers a chain: internal staff coordination + customer WhatsApp message (manual "are you okay to move?" → wait for yes → then move). No automation today.
- Staff don't reply fast on WhatsApp when urgent → Queenie physically walks up to the stylist.

**Deposits, inconsistent by design**
- Deposit % varies by service: **hair/nails 25%, facials/makeup/SPMU (semi-permanent makeup) 50%** (higher no-show risk / higher cost).
- VIP/regulars often **not asked** for a deposit (relationship call).

**Hair extensions & hair color = consultation-gated, can't be booked clean**
- No exact price; only "starts from." Sends price list + pushes a **consultation** always. Extensions priced per pack (~1250 to 1550 AED/pack by shade) + fitting + color. Quotes a padded range to be safe.
- Hair color also consultation-first; choosing services is hard, asks for photos of desired look + current hair.
- Hair director changes bookings after consult. Queenie pushes same-day service after consult to avoid losing the client.

**Duplicate / double-booking problem (recurring)**
- Clients book via multiple channels (WhatsApp, **Instagram** handled by a different person, direct-to-stylist) → duplicates and real-time collisions booked manually.
- Clients reschedule without saying they already have a booking. Queenie wants **duplicate detection + a future-appointments tab + alerts** ("this person seems to have two appointments today").
- Covering staff on Queenie's day off lack the original conversation context.

**Regulars & recurring patterns**
- Standing bookings: blow-dry every Tue/Wed weekly "book me until December"; nails every 3 to 4 weeks; some book months/half-year ahead because the technician is always back-to-back.
- Notes hacks: "times 2" for mom/sister on one number; family/friends comps.

**Staff autonomy / data-quality noise**
- Stylists have calendar access: can **book, change price, discount to zero** (friends, content). "Staff hair color" = internal free-service tracking.
- Stylists also block time to avoid taking clients; some don't use block-time (Christine's rule), so they "drag" appointments manually instead.

## Build philosophy stated (Cami → Queenie)

- Start small, one step at a time; solve the single biggest problem, then move. Piecemeal.
- Not aiming for a fully autonomous WhatsApp, human stays in the scheduling loop. It's a **CRM inbox that reads + prompts**, Queenie approves ("you pressed it, it worked").
- Aim: manage everything from the conversation, no back-and-forth to the calendar.
- "Bad feedback is good feedback", explicitly invited.
